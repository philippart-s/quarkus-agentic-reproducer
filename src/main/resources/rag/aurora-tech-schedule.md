# AuroraTech Summit 2027

## Overview

The **AuroraTech Summit 2027** is a fictional, invitation-only technology conference focused on emerging systems architecture, applied AI governance, and experimental developer tooling.

- **Location:** Reykjavik, Iceland
- **Date:** September 14, 2027
- **Audience:** Senior engineers, architects, research leads
- **Format:** Single-day, single-track conference

---

## Conference Theme

**"Engineering Systems That Outlive Their Creators"**

The conference explores long-term software maintainability, decision traceability, and knowledge preservation in complex technical systems.

---

## Schedule – September 14, 2027

### 09:00 – Opening Keynote

**Speaker:** Dr. Elena Vorvik  
**Title:** *Software That Ages Gracefully*

**Abstract:**  
This keynote discusses design principles for software systems intended to remain operational and understandable for more than 20 years, with real-world failure anecdotes from large-scale infrastructure projects.

---

### 10:30 – Session 1

**Speaker:** Marcus H. Kline  
**Title:** *The Hidden Cost of Implicit Architecture*

**Topics Covered:**
- Architectural decisions that are never documented
- Long-term impact of undocumented conventions
- Techniques for making architectural intent explicit

---

### 13:00 – Session 2

**Speaker:** Amina Solberg  
**Title:** *Governing AI Without Freezing Innovation*

**Topics Covered:**
- Lightweight governance models for internal AI tools
- Auditability vs. developer velocity
- Case study: the "Glass Ledger" decision log framework

---

### 15:00 – Session 3

**Speaker:** Tobias Renner  
**Title:** *RAG Systems Beyond Question Answering*

**Topics Covered:**
- Using Retrieval-Augmented Generation for system archaeology
- RAG-driven incident postmortems
- Non-obvious failure modes in long-lived RAG pipelines

---

### 16:30 – Closing Talk

**Speaker:** Dr. Elena Vorvik  
**Title:** *Designing for Unknown Future Users*

**Abstract:**  
A closing reflection on designing systems for users who may not yet exist, including future engineers, auditors, and autonomous agents.

---

## Notable Constraints

- No sessions were recorded.

---

## Internal Terminology

- **Glass Ledger:** An internal decision-record format introduced during Session 2.
- **System Archaeology:** A term used in Session 3 to describe reconstructing system intent using logs, documents, and RAG pipelines.
